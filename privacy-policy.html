Last Updated: April 4, 2026

Welcome to Unique Orb. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Third-Party Service Providers

Our application uses third-party service providers to display advertisements and perform analytics. These services may collect data from your device to personalize ads and measure performance.

  • Google AdMob: Privacy Policy

2. Data Collection

Through AdMob, the following information may be collected:

  • Advertising ID: Android Advertising ID (AAID).
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and screen size.
  • Interaction Data: Ad clicks, impressions, and interaction duration.
  • Approximate Location: General location data derived from your IP address.

3. Cookies and Advertising Identifiers

Users can opt-out of personalized advertising or reset their advertising ID through their device settings (Google Settings > Ads). AdMob may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance the in-app advertising experience and provide relevant content.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our application does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. During ad delivery, restricted data processing modes provided by AdMob are utilized for content directed toward children to ensure compliance with privacy regulations.
